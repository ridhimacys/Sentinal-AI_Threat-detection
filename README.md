# SENTINEL-AI

### Social Media Intelligence & Cybersecurity Threat Detection Platform

SENTINEL-AI is an AI-powered social media intelligence platform designed to analyze social-media activity and identify suspicious, harmful, and coordinated behaviour.

Instead of looking only at sentiment or trends, SENTINEL-AI combines social-media analytics with cybersecurity-oriented behavioural and coordination analysis to provide explainable threat intelligence.

## Problem

Large volumes of social-media activity make it difficult for analysts to manually identify:

- Coordinated activity across multiple accounts
- Suspicious behavioural patterns
- Cyberbullying and harassment
- Sudden activity bursts
- Manipulative campaigns
- Emerging harmful trends
- Relationships between accounts, hashtags and URLs

Traditional social-media analytics can show **what people are saying**, but additional analysis is required to understand **how, when, and whether multiple accounts are behaving together**.



## Solution

SENTINEL-AI follows a multi-layer analysis approach:

Social Media Data -> Data Collection & Cleaning -> Social Intelligence + Cybersecurity Analysis -> Behaviour & Coordination Analysis -> Campaign & Risk Intelligence -> Threat Score + Evidence -> Investigation Dashboard  

## Key Features

- Social Media Analytics — Sentiment, emotions, topics, trends and audience behaviour
- Cyberbullying Detection — Identifies potentially harmful and harassing activity
- Coordination Fingerprinting — Analyzes text similarity, shared hashtags/URLs and timing patterns
- Behavioural Anomaly Detection — Identifies unusual activity bursts and suspicious behaviour
- Threat Risk Score — Combines multiple signals into an explainable 0–100 risk indicator
- Historical Campaign Matching — Compares current activity with historical threat patterns
- Network Intelligence — Maps relationships between accounts, hashtags, URLs and campaigns
- Investigation Dashboard — Presents alerts, evidence, risk indicators and relationships
